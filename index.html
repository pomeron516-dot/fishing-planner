    Rules and season notes

    • Bluefish, NJ 2026: 5 per person from shore or a private boat, 7 on a licensed party or charter boat. No size limit, no closed season.
    • Black sea bass, NJ: up to 10 fish from Sept 23 to Oct 31 (12.5 in. minimum as of the May forecast). Party boats are likely to run bottom trips now, so call and ask about bluefish.
    • Fluke: NJ season closed Sept 25. Tautog: one fish through October.
    • Albies: late September into October is prime on the NJ beaches. Eastern Long Island Sound and Montauk usually hold them longer.
    • Albie and bonito rules were not checked for this build. Confirm current regulations before you go.
    How scores work

    Fish odds combine a seasonal curve for each species in each area with how well each spot suits it. With the kids setting on, odds also reward places that keep rods bent with any species, such as party boats that can switch to bottom fishing. Recent reports are weighted by age and pulled toward neutral when they are old or missing. Tide and light score how much moving water and dawn or dusk your window covers, using NOAA tide predictions for the nearest station. Weather uses the live marine forecast, or the wind and seas you type in. Drive scores drop as travel time approaches your limit. The weights between these factors are built into the model and are not adjustable. Schedule, drive and boat length limits are hard filters. Charters get a 15% penalty. A day is called a no-go when the best trip scores under 55 or the forecast looks rough. Drive times are straight-line estimates scaled for roads, so check the map link before you commit.
